The test runs two real Node processes against one SQLite database, checks exactly one loser, resolves the undispatched claim, then crashes a third process after a simulated provider write. Deleting it, mocking its processes, or raising its deadline without reproducing the cause would lose evidence or conceal uncertainty. No fix is claimed.
