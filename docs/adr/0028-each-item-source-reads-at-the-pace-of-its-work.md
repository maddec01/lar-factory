# Each item source reads at the pace of its work

The board reads each item source on its own: every 15 seconds while it holds active work, every five minutes otherwise, and at once when the board, `status.sh` or `slice.sh` names an item it lists. Work is active while its T3 thread, or one of its slices' threads, runs or ran in the last five minutes, while it sits in an agent's status and T3 can't say, while it closed in the last five minutes, while its PR's checks run, while the board shows a change to it that the provider hasn't reported, and while an armed start waits on it or for it. Repository configs and other metadata are read on the idle pace and on an explicit refresh only. A rate-limited `gh` or `az` makes no calls until its limit resets, and a GitHub quota under a fifth keeps every source at the idle pace. T3's threads and stats refresh from its database alone.

Reading the whole board every 45 seconds spent most calls on work nobody was changing and still ran into GitHub's limits. Reading by activity keeps the work agents are changing current, while edits made in a provider's web UI to idle items can take up to five minutes to show. We chose this over an operation journal with recovery and per-board notifications. Lar-work's scripts already write through the provider and ask the boards for a read, so a lost request costs one idle interval, not correctness.
